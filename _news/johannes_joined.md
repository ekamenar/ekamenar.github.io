Johannes Heeg, a PhD student at the Optimization-based Control group at <a href="https://www.tu-ilmenau.de/">TU Ilmenau</a>, joined our research group at the University of Rijeka as a visiting researcher. Welcome Johannes!

During his two-week stay, Johannes will work on Koopman operator-based modeling and control. The visit provides an opportunity to deepen his understanding of Koopman-based methods by exploring their application to soft robotics.

We are excited to welcome Johannes to Rijeka and look forward to a productive and insightful time together!
