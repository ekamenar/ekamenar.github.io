We are pleased to announce the third edition of the international workshop <a href="https://y-susuki.github.io/kot3/">"Koopman Operator Theory: Fundamentals, Approximations and Applications 3"</a>!

---

The workshop will be held from **December 11th to 13th, 2026** at the **Clock Tower Centennial Hall, Kyoto University, Japan**.

The workshop will bring together researchers working on dynamical systems, data-driven modeling, computation, control, and applications of Koopman operator theory across science and technology.

**Organizing Committee:**

```text
Yoshihiko Susuki (Kyoto University, Japan) — Chair
Igor Mezić (University of California, USA)
Ervin Kamenar (University of Rijeka, Croatia)
Alexandre Mauroy (University of Namur, Belgium)
Hiroya Nakao (Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan)
Antonio Navarra (University of Bologna, Italy)
```

This third edition follows the previous workshops held in **Otranto, Italy (2024)** and **Cres, Croatia (2025)**. The workshop will focus on the fundamentals, approximations, and applications of Koopman operator theory, including spectral foundations, reliable computational and data-driven methods, and emerging applications in modeling and control.

The event will provide an international forum for exchanging recent results, discussing open research challenges, and establishing new collaborations across the growing Koopman operator community.

More information about the program, confirmed speakers, venue, travel, and accommodation can be found on the official workshop website: <a href="https://y-susuki.github.io/kot3/">Koopman Operator Theory: Fundamentals, Approximations and Applications 3</a>.

Members of our team will present recent results from the Croatian Science Foundation (**HRZZ**) project <a href="https://ekamenar.github.io/projects/HRZZ_2024_ASoRo/">Advanced Soft Robots: Data-Driven Development, Modeling and Control</a>.
