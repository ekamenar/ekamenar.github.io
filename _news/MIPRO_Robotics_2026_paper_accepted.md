Our paper **“Bridging Rehabilitation Requirements and Soft Robotics: Concept and Design of a Pneumatic Assistive Device”** has been accepted for presentation at **MIPRO Robotics 2026**.

---

The paper presents our **preliminary research toward the development of a soft pneumatic assistive device for hand rehabilitation**. The work focuses on translating rehabilitation-oriented requirements—such as safety, comfort, functional finger assistance, manufacturability, and reliability—into practical engineering design decisions.

Several actuator and fabrication concepts were investigated, including a rigid tendon-driven baseline, molded elastomer actuators, reinforced pneumatic actuators, 3D-printed TPU structures, and initial vat-photopolymerized soft actuators. The study also summarizes recurring practical challenges such as **air leakage, rupture, connector degradation, and material limitations**, which are important considerations when moving soft robotic devices from concepts toward reliable wearable systems.

The mechanical development is supported by **hand kinematic analysis and FEM/PRB-based modeling**, while future work will extend the platform toward adaptive assistance using **sEMG-based intent estimation and Koopman-based data-driven modeling and control**.

This work represents an **early stage of our research within the HRZZ project ASoRo – Advanced Soft Robots: Data-Driven Development, Modeling and Control**, and provides a foundation for subsequent actuator characterization, fabrication improvements, sensing integration, and closed-loop rehabilitation experiments.

The paper will be presented at **MIPRO Robotics 2026 in Zadar, Croatia**.
